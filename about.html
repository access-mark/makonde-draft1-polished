About Makonde

Local accountability. Specialist capability.

Makonde connects South African organisations with practical enterprise technology solutions through specialist partnerships.

Our story

Established to make complex technology useful

Makonde was formed in 2023 to connect South African organisations with specialist technologies that address urgent enterprise challenges. As a 100% Black female-owned IT-focused company, Makonde brings together local engagement, commercial accountability and global technology capability.

Our approach starts with understanding the business outcome, not presenting a catalogue of products.

Our mission

Help organisations improve efficiency, security and decision-making through practical, high-impact technology solutions.

Our vision

Become a trusted African technology partner for secure, intelligent and sustainable digital transformation.
